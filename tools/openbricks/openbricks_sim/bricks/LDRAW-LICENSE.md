# LDraw parts library attribution

`technic_bundle.json.zlib` is derived from the LDraw parts library
(https://www.ldraw.org), which is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution licences CC BY 2.0 and CC BY 4.0. The geometry of every
part in the bundle is a conversion of the corresponding LDraw part
file; the list of parts is `technic_parts.txt`, and
`openbricks_sim/bricks/ldraw.py` is the converter, so the bundle can
be regenerated from any LDraw library release.

Part weights in `weights.json` are catalogue facts from BrickLink,
recorded with their source in each record's `source_note`.
